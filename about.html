About Me

Who I am, where I am from, and what I am working toward.

I am a second-year Computer Science student at AUT, studying Software Development with minors in AI and Digital Services. My approach to building software is straightforward: identify a real problem, understand it properly, then work backwards to the right solution.

I am proud to be Māori, Samoan and Tongan. I whakapapa to Ngāti Māahanga, part of the Waikato Tainui iwi and my Pasifika roots are from Salelologa and Kolomotu'a. I am motivated by the opportunity to create meaningful tools for Maori and Pasifika communities in tech. Whether that is building better infrastructure or exploring how AI could support us in the future, I want the work I do to connect to something real.

My long-term goal is solutions architecture: designing systems end to end, consulting with customers and sitting at the intersection of engineering and commercial strategy. To get there I am building technical depth through projects and AWS certification, and commercial awareness through real work experience.

Outside of code I play every sport under the sun: basketball, volleyball, motocross, golf, football and rugby just to name a few. Team sport has taught me how to communicate under pressure, lead without authority and stay accountable when it counts. I am also a big fan of chess and strategy games, which I find are a great way to exercise problem-solving in a different context.
